# Prep results: toy mechanisms only (not an evaluation of actual signals)

Generated by `run_experiment.py` (full run). All images are synthetic, non-intimate procedural landscapes and geometric shapes. **No real/fake verdicts are issued.** The manifest is a toy format (not C2PA) and the watermark is a toy scheme (not SynthID or any product). These results are preparation only. They do not evaluate actual provenance, watermark or detector signals; see docs/ACTUAL_SIGNAL_ROUTES.md.

## Unevaluated

- **AI-image detectors:** none available offline. No detector scores were produced or estimated.
- **C2PA verification:** no C2PA tool installed. C2PA conformance was not evaluated.
